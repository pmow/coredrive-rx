# CoreDrive RX v1.8.0

- **Asking a repeater that is already a saved contact now works.** Its stored `out_path`
  could differ from the zero-hop link node-discover had just confirmed, so `sendAnonReq`
  flooded or source-routed the request and no reply could come. The contact is forced to
  zero-hop for the ask and always restored afterwards — including after a crash or BLE
  drop, via a replay on the next connect.
